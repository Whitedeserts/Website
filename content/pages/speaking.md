# Speaking

I regularly give lectures, workshops, and conference presentations on GeoAI, remote sensing, spatial data science, and applied AI. My speaking work is grounded in the same principle as my technical work: make advanced methods understandable enough that people can evaluate them, adapt them, and use them responsibly. The goal is for participants to leave with a working understanding of both the technology and the judgment needed to apply it well.

## Experience

- 50+ invited lectures, technical workshops, and conference presentations
- Delivered sessions across 10+ Canadian universities, government agencies, and national and international events
- Experience speaking to technical teams, students, researchers, executives, government partners, and mixed public audiences

## Speaking Topics

- GeoAI and the future of intelligent GIS
- Geospatial foundation models
- Deep learning and computer vision for Earth observation
- Generative AI, RAG and AI agents for geospatial workflows
- From AI prototype to operational geospatial system
- Teaching GIS in the age of AI

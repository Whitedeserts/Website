# Writing

I write to make GeoAI and applied machine learning easier to understand, evaluate, and use. My technical writing is usually aimed at people who need to connect research ideas with real geospatial systems: GIS professionals, researchers, students, government teams, and technical leaders.

## Topics I Write About

- GeoAI and geospatial deep learning
- Foundation models for Earth observation and GIS
- Agentic AI, GenAI, RAG, and enterprise automation
- Remote sensing workflows for satellite and aerial imagery
- Python, ArcGIS, reproducible spatial analytics, and practical MLOps
- Technical communication for mixed technical and non-technical audiences

## Editorial Approach

Good technical writing should reduce friction. I aim for writing that explains the problem, shows the workflow, names the tradeoffs, and gives readers enough context to adapt the idea to their own data and organization.
